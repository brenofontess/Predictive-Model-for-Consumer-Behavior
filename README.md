# Predictive-Model-for-Consumer-Behavior

**Objective:** Forecast the probability of future customer purchases by mapping purchase patterns and translating complex predictive model outputs into actionable business insights and KPIs.

### 📊 Data Engineering & EDA
* **Dataset:** Customer data sourced from Kaggle datasets.
* **ETL Pipeline:** Developed and executed pipelines to clean and transform raw data.
* **Database Migration:** Ensured data integrity during the migration to a PostgreSQL relational database.
* **EDA:** Applied analytical frameworks to define project objectives and map customer purchasing behaviors.

### 🤖 Predictive Modeling
* **Environment:** Developed in R.
* **Techniques Applied:** Statistical learning techniques, specifically Classification and Clustering algorithms.
* **Core Output:** Calculated probability scores for future customer purchases.

### 💡 Business Insights & Visualization
* **Dashboards:** Designed interactive dashboards using Power BI.
* **Business Translation:** Successfully translated complex mathematical model outputs into accessible, actionable business metrics and KPIs for decision-makers.
